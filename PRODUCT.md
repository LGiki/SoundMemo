# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need to capture, review, and manage voice recordings quickly on an Android device. They often use the app one-handed and need the recording state and primary action to be immediately understandable.

## Product Purpose

SoundMemo is a local-first voice recorder for dependable capture, playback, and file management without accounts, advertising, analytics, or cloud sync. Success means a user can start recording, understand its state, and find or share a saved recording with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and private. The interface should feel like a focused Android system tool: confident enough for serious use, quiet enough to stay out of the way.

## Anti-references

Avoid audio-editor complexity, decorative recording-studio skeuomorphism, aggressive alarm-red surfaces, and settings screens that feel like a dense legacy preference list. Do not use visual noise or animation to compete with the recording state.

## Design Principles

1. Put the recording state and next safe action first.
2. Make local-first privacy feel dependable and unobtrusive.
3. Use familiar Material 3 patterns consistently across recorder, library, and settings.
4. Preserve clarity in light, dark, and dynamic-color themes.
5. Scale gracefully from compact phones to larger Android screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow Material 3 defaults with readable text, semantic labels, sufficient color contrast, and standard Android touch targets. Never rely on color alone to communicate recording or playback state.
