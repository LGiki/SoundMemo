# Product

SoundMemo is an open source Android voice recorder for everyday audio capture: meetings, lectures, interviews, personal notes, practice sessions, reminders, and field recordings.

The v1.0 implementation focuses on:

- One-tap recording from the main screen.
- Reliable local save using M4A/AAC.
- Foreground service recording while the app is backgrounded.
- Searchable recording library.
- Rename, share, delete, restore, and permanent deletion.
- Basic Media3 playback with seeking and speed controls.
- Material 3 UI with light, dark, and dynamic color support.

Non-goals for v1.0 include cloud sync, online transcription, AI summarization, collaborative sharing, complex audio editing, multi-track recording, accounts, ads, and monetization SDKs.
